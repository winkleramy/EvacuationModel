Evacuation Modeling

Why this work matters

When many households leave at once, narrow roads and busy merges can create long queues. This project explores how traffic could move through our shared road network during an evacuation, helping our community identify bottlenecks, quantify risk, and test solutions.

How the model works

A computer simulation moves household vehicles through connected roads and intersections. Departure timing, vehicle count, and evacuation route information are taken from our Evacuation Modeling Survey. The simulation models each vehicle as it traverses its own evacuation route, considering stop and merge rules, single-lane constraints, and priority for emergency vehicles.

Your local knowledge makes the model better

Share how many vehicles your household would use, how long you would need to get ready, your primary and secondary routes, and any road or access issues you know about via our Evacuation Modeling Survey.

What the model shows

  • Estimated evacuation times: How long modeled traffic takes to clear the road network.
  • Traffic bottlenecks: Where queues build up and constrain traffic flow.
  • Departure timing: How the spread of household departures changes evacuation and commute times.
